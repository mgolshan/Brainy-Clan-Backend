## Partner with Brainy Clan

For parents and creators who share our mission: kids using screens to learn and grow rather than
waste their time and mind. Recommend Brainy Clan to other parents with your own code and earn a
commission on paid plans.

## What Brainy Clan is

A family learning app. Here is what you can say about it — every point is true today:

- Quizzes, Sudoku (4×4 to 9×9), Math Sprint, word puzzles (anagrams, missing letters, word search)
  and brain games (memory match, pattern sequence, nonograms, spot the difference).
- Everyone plays at their own level: questions adapt to each player's age group and difficulty.
- Family goals (daily, weekly, monthly): race each other or team up for a real-world reward.
- Kids join with just a nickname — no email, no password, no real name, no photo. A parent gives
  consent for each child.
- No ads for children, ever. No contact with strangers: the only chat is your own private family
  chat, and parents can turn it off.
- Every question is reviewed before it is published. English and French, on iPhone, iPad and Android.

## How it works

1. **Apply** — email us with your channels and audience. We check that your content speaks to
   parents and fits our mission.
2. **Get your code** — we create your personal code (e.g. `MAYA`) and your link
   `brainyclan.com/r/YOURCODE`.
3. **Share with #ad** — tell parents about Brainy Clan, with a clear disclosure in every post.
4. **Earn** — when a family uses your code and pays for the Family plan, you earn a commission on
   the terms below.

## What your audience gets

The free plan stays free: anyone can try Brainy Clan without paying. Your code links the family to
you. **If your code includes an offer**, your audience also gets it:

- an App Store or Google Play offer code, redeemed when buying in the app;
- a discount applied automatically when they subscribe on brainyclan.com through your link or code
  (only once web checkout is open — the website shows this line only then).

We tell you which offer your code includes when we create it. Never promise a discount we have not
confirmed.

## Commission and payouts

Standard terms (your written agreement may set different terms; it takes precedence):

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **25%** of net revenue | from each payment of a family linked to your code. Net = the price paid minus sales tax and the store or Paddle fee. |
| **12 months** per family | counted from the family's first payment, renewals included. |
| **30-day** refund hold | each commission waits out the refund window before it becomes payable. Refunds and chargebacks are deducted. |
| **Monthly** payout | we pay by hand once a month, with a statement by email. |

Currencies: CAD and USD (the currency of the sale). No minimum payout is set today.

### How you get paid

- By PayPal, Interac e-Transfer (Canada) or bank transfer, sent by hand by our team.
- Your statement shows amounts per month and currency, never who the families are.
- You handle your own taxes and invoices. We may ask for an invoice or tax details where the law
  requires it.
- Never email us a card number or a full account number: we agree on a safe way together.

## The rules

Brainy Clan is an app for families. These rules protect children, your audience and you.

**Always**

- Disclose in every post, at the start and clearly: "#ad" or "Paid partnership with Brainy Clan",
  and say it out loud in videos (FTC, Competition Bureau, Ad Standards).
- Speak to parents and adults.
- Use only the claims in "What Brainy Clan is" and the brand kit.
- Stay true to our mission and content rules: positive, honest and educational, with no political,
  ideological or religious messaging.
- Film a child only with their parents' written consent, and never show a child's name or face in
  the app.

**Never**

- Target or address children, or post on kid-directed channels.
- Make false, medical or guaranteed-grades claims ("better grades guaranteed", "proven"), or mention
  certifications we don't hold.
- Spam or send unsolicited messages.
- Bid on "Brainy Clan" in search ads.
- Post fake reviews, offer incentivised installs, use cookie stuffing, or use your own code
  (self-referrals).

If these rules are broken, we may end the partnership and withhold the related commission.

## Frequently asked questions

**How does attribution work?** A family is linked to the first partner code it uses: the first code
wins. The code must be used before the family's first payment; after that it is locked. We use no
cookies and no tracking, so a family is credited only when your code is actually used.

**What about App Store and Google Play purchases?** They count when the parent entered your code in
the app (at sign-up or under "Have a code?") before paying, or redeemed a store offer code linked to
your code. Once web checkout is open, your link also fills in the code on the website's subscribe
form.

**When do I get paid?** A commission becomes payable after the refund window (30 days), then it is
paid with the next payout (once a month).

**Can I see my stats?** You get a monthly statement by email (amounts per month and currency). A
stats page is planned. We never share who the families are.

**What happens with refunds?** If a family gets a refund or a chargeback, the commission on that
payment is reversed and deducted from your next payout.

**Can I use my own code?** No. Self-referrals don't earn commission.
